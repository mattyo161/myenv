# Design Decisions

Append-only decision log (ADR-lite). Add an entry whenever a decision changes
a contract, an architecture, or a convention in this repo. Strike through
superseded entries with a pointer to their replacement — never delete.

## Decision log template

```markdown
### <Short imperative title>
<What was decided and why — 2-6 sentences. Name the alternative(s) rejected
and the constraint that drove the choice. If this supersedes an earlier
entry, strike that entry through and link here.>
```
